§6 · Building an ROI case

Time saved vs. token spend vs. defect reduction

A credible ROI case counts what AI adds, not only what it saves: review time, token spend, seats, and the people who maintain the rules, skills and gates. The defect effect can go either way. Start from your own measured baseline.

📌 Recap

From a capstone to a practice

  • Use the least autonomous interaction that does the job. Don’t hand an agent anything you can’t specify, verify, reverse, or be accountable for.
  • Verify on four dimensions: correctness against the spec, security, maintainability and traceability. Automate every check you can.
  • Roll out in phases: pilot, paved road, scale, sustain. Guardrails are central; standards and grounding stay with the teams.
  • Model cost per call and per task. Grounding by retrieval, prompt caching and fewer correction rounds are the main levers.
  • An ROI case counts review time, tokens, seats, maintenance and the defect effect, starts from a measured baseline, and shows its sensitivity.
  • Every shared AI asset has an owner, a version, a review path, a regression check and a way to retire it. Maintenance is a lifecycle, not a handoff.
