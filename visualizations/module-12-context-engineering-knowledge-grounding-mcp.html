📌 Recap

Day 4 starts here: what your agents are allowed to know

  • Context engineering is three deliberate moves — select, structure, and scope — not "paste in everything."
  • Grounded claims trace back to one of five sources: repository code, SRS/SDS documents, standards, historical defects, or enterprise knowledge.
  • MCP separates Resources (read-only retrieval) from Tools (actions) — that separation is the guardrail, not an implementation detail.
  • Retrieval strategy should match the question: semantic search, structured lookup, or graph traversal.
  • A "no unsupported facts" guardrail means citing sources for every claim and declining out-of-scope questions instead of guessing.
  • The right context source depends on the task: direct repository context, a repo-independent rule/skill, or an MCP connection to an external system.
